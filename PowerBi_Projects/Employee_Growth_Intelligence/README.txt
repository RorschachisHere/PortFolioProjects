📊 Employee Growth Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)

This project is a multi-page Power BI dashboard built to analyze employee performance, track month-over-month growth, and identify top and low performers across departments.

The goal was to move beyond basic reporting and create something that actually helps answer real questions like:

Who is consistently improving?
Who is falling behind?
Are employees meeting performance thresholds?
How does performance change over time?

🚀 What this dashboard covers

1. Executive Overview

A high-level snapshot of overall performance:

Total employees vs eligible employees
Current vs previous performance comparison
Month-over-month growth trends
2× target achievement tracking

This page is designed for quick decision-making without digging into details.

2. Department Analysis

Breaks performance down by:

Department → Team → Employee (drilldown enabled)
Trend of output over time by department
Average MoM growth comparison across departments

This helps identify which areas of the organization are improving and which need attention.

3. Employee Insights

Focused on individual performance:

Top performer and lowest performer identification
Highest MoM growth tracking
Top 10 and Bottom 10 employees by performance
Clear ranking-based visuals for quick comparison

This page is built to highlight both excellence and risk areas.

🧠 Key Features

Interactive slicers (Year-Month filtering across pages)
Dynamic DAX measures (MoM Growth, Rankings, Ratios)
Top N filtering for performance comparison
Clean, minimal layout focused on readability
Consistent formatting across all visuals

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI (Data modeling, DAX, visualization)
DAX for calculations:
MoM Growth %
Ranking (Top/Bottom performers)
Target achievement logic
Data modeling with dimension tables:
Date
Employee
Department / Team

📌 What I focused on

Instead of just adding visuals, I focused on:

Keeping the layout clean and uncluttered
Making insights easy to understand in seconds
Avoiding unnecessary charts that don’t add value
Building something that looks professional and usable in a real business setting
